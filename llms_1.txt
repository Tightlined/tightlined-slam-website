# The Tightlined Slam

> A fly and light tackle, catch-and-release fishing tournament on the western Long Island Sound, benefiting fisheries conservation. Organized by the Tightlined Conservation Coalition, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

## Key facts

- **Event:** The 9th Annual Tightlined Slam
- **Dates:** October 16–18, 2026
- **Location:** Western Long Island Sound (boundaries: Throgs Neck Bridge to the east of Mount Sinai Harbor / Branford cable crossing area)
- **Format:** Catch-and-release only, fly and light tackle (spin) divisions
- **Categories:** Nine competition categories — striped bass, bluefish, and hard tails (false albacore/bonito) each split by fly and spin, plus a Slam category and a Team Champion category
- **New for 2026:** The Generations Division, a parent/child team category introduced in honor of Charlie Erdmann
- **Included with registration:** The Block Party — oysters, drinks, music, raffle, and awards ceremony
- **Organizer:** Tightlined Conservation Coalition, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Lyme, CT
- **Contact:** tayloringraham@tightlined.com / (203) 856-9750
